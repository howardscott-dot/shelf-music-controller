# Third-party services and assets

SHELF connects to products and services owned by their respective providers. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify, Jellyfin, Plex, WiiM, Naim, Cambridge Audio, Denon, Marantz, Yamaha, Linn, Sony, Pioneer, Onkyo, MusicBrainz or the Cover Art Archive.

- The Spotify logo in `apps/web/public` is an official Spotify brand asset. Its use remains subject to Spotify's branding requirements; see the adjacent source note.
- Spotify album artwork is supplied at runtime by Spotify and is not included in this repository.
- Jellyfin library artwork belongs to its respective rights holders and is not included in this repository.
- Plex library artwork is supplied by the user's own Plex Media Server and is not included in this repository.
- Packaging scans are requested at runtime from the Cover Art Archive. Availability does not grant additional rights beyond those attached to the source material.
- Inter, DM Mono and Patrick Hand SC are loaded from Google Fonts and are distributed under their respective font licences.
- `music-metadata` and its dependencies are used under their respective open-source licences to read tags from user-provided audio files.

Product and service names may be trademarks of their respective owners.
